01 — The apology

Noor, I'm truly sorry.

I know I hurt you, and I know that a simple “sorry” cannot undo what I did. I cheated, and that was my choice. There isn't an excuse that makes it okay. You trusted me, and I didn't respect that trust the way you deserved.

I'm sorry for the pain, the doubts, the overthinking, and every moment I made you question whether you were enough. You were never the problem. I was responsible for my actions, and I need to own them without asking you to make me feel better about them.

I don't expect you to forget anything just because I'm saying these words now. I understand that trust takes time, and I understand if these words are difficult to believe.

“I can't change the way I treated you before. I can only take responsibility for it and show, through my choices, who I am trying to become.”
02 — What I wish you knew

I don't want to play with your feelings.

I'm not here because I'm bored, lonely, or looking for temporary attention. I'm here because I've thought about what happened, what I lost, and the way I treated someone who mattered to me.

I miss you, Noor. I miss talking to you, laughing with you, and having you in my day. But I also know missing you doesn't give me the right to ask for you back. Your feelings matter just as much as mine, and your peace matters too.

I want to start again only if you genuinely want that too — not because you feel guilty, pressured, or afraid of hurting me.

03 — If you ever let me try again

I want my actions to mean more than my promises.

HonestyNo hiding things, no half-truths, and no excuses when a conversation gets difficult.
ConsistencyNot just being caring for a day. Showing respect through ordinary days and difficult ones.
RespectListening to your boundaries and accepting your feelings, even when they're hard for me to hear.
PatienceNot demanding instant trust. Letting you decide what feels safe and right for you.

I know promises are easy to type. I don't expect you to trust a list on a website. If you ever choose to give me another chance, I know I would have to earn that trust slowly.

05 — One last thing

Would you ever consider one more chance?

I am serious, Noor, and I don't want to waste your time or play with your feelings again. Take your time. Think about what you want, without any pressure from me.

If there's still a part of you that wants to try, maybe we can begin slowly — not pretend nothing happened, but build something healthier with honesty and respect. And if your answer is no, I'll accept it. I won't argue with your feelings.

I'm not asking you to trust this page. I'm asking you to judge me by what I do from now on.

Noor, I'm sorry. I wish I had understood the value of your trust before I hurt you. I can't ask you to erase the past, but I can promise to take responsibility for it. Take all the time you need. Whatever you decide, thank you for reading this. — From me
